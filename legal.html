Who is responsible. The operator named above. Because the study is reachable from the European Economic Area, this notice is written to satisfy the General Data Protection Regulation as well as United States law; where the GDPR applies, the operator is the controller within the meaning of Article 4(7).

What the study is, technically. A set of static files: a page, two scripts, a stylesheet, data files and images. It runs entirely in your browser. There is no server-side program, no account, no login, no form, and nothing you decide is ever sent anywhere; the model runs on your own device. The study sets no cookies, uses no analytics or advertising service, calls no artificial-intelligence service, and loads nothing from third-party servers: fonts are those installed on your device, images and data come from this site itself.

One entry on your device. So that you can continue where you stopped, the study keeps your mode, your decisions and a random seed in your browser's local storage, under the name twoflyingboats_state. Nothing else is stored. The entry contains no personal information and is never sent anywhere; it persists until you clear this site's data or start again. If your browser blocks local storage, the study still works but forgets your decisions when the tab is closed.
